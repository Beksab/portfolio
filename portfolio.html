Exit Exam Simulator

#html • #education • #webdevelopment

A comprehensive platform helping graduating students prepare for the national exit exam. The simulator covers five departments: Management, Logistics and Supply Chain Management, Accounting and Finance, Journalism and Communication, and Law. Each department contains up to 15 courses with 100+ questions each, plus comprehensive questions for thorough preparation.

Why I included this: This project demonstrates my ability to build practical, user-friendly web solutions that address real educational needs.
