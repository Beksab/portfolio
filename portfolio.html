Exit Exam Simulator

#html • #education • #webdevelopment

A comprehensive platform helping graduating students prepare for the national exit exam. The simulator covers five departments:

  • Department of Management
  • Department of Logistics and Supply Chain Management
  • Department of Accounting and Finance
  • Department of Journalism and Communication
  • Department of Law

Each department contains up to 15 courses with 100+ questions each, plus comprehensive questions for thorough preparation.

Why I included this: This project demonstrates my ability to build practical, user-friendly web solutions that address real educational needs.
